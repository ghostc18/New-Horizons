Questions

Straight answers

Is a cash offer lower than listing?

Often yes. You trade some price for speed, certainty, no repairs, no showings, no commissions, and no holding costs.

Do I clean or fix anything?

No. We buy as-is. Repairs are factored into the offer — you don't do them.

How fast can we close?

As soon as 14 days if title is clear. You pick the date.

Am I locked in after asking?

No. No obligation. You see the offer, then decide.

What if I have a mortgage or liens?

We pay them off at closing from the proceeds. You walk with the net.

Can I stay after closing?

Yes. Rent-backs are common — we build it into the terms.
